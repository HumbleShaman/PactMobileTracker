# Live view (scriptable/live.html)

Scope: the tracker's single page, on the iPhone (Scriptable WebView) and desktop (GitHub Pages). Mode: Operate. Five tabs: Overview, Flow, Pools, Tokens, vs Tinyman. Sound and big-fee moments are on by default behind one switch beside the theme button (see DESIGN.md, Sound & Big-Fee Moments); no odometer, toasts or idle animation.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Pact's order flow drawn as a transit network: each source (Hay router, NKBD arb, Q6WW, RCFX bot, Deflex, Vestige, other bots, wallets) is a coloured line running into the pool stations it trades. Refuses the stat-card grid with generic line charts.

OWN-WORLD: Map-paper ground (#f3f3f0) and white sheets with hairlines; dark mode graphite (#0d0d0d / #1a1a19). A black signage band carries the title, live status, ALGO price and tabs. Helvetica Neue / system sans, tabular figures in tables. Line bullets (coloured discs with a white letter) mark every actor wherever it appears.

STORY: Glance the month's fees and block rewards; open Flow to see who sent today's volume and where it went; check Pools and Tokens for where money moves; use vs Tinyman to find pairs Pact is missing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Band and tabs on top. Overview: November fees as the one hero figure with protocol/LP split and today, the month-vs-last-month pace chart, a 24h line-share strip, and the live swaps list (right column on desktop, below on phone).
